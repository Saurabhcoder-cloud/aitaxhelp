## 3. Discovered Issues & Root Causes

During initial execution of the Phase 1 test suite, 12 test assertions failed across the 20 scenarios. Root cause analysis revealed 5 distinct issues:

### Issue 1: Statutory Standard Deduction for Tax Year 2025 (`RULES_2025`)
- **Symptom:** Scenarios 1, 3, 4, and 6 failed standard deduction assertions (e.g. expected 15,000.00 / 30,000.00 / 22,500.00, received 15,750.00 / 31,500.00 / 23,625.00).
- **Root Cause:** In the authoritative tax engine `tax-engine/rules/2025/index.ts` (IRS IRB 2025-45 / P.L. 119-21 OBBBA), the statutory 2025 standard deductions are $15,750 (Single/MFS), $31,500 (MFJ/QSS), and $23,625 (HOH). The test mock had hardcoded unadjusted round numbers ($15k / $30k / $22.5k).
- **Resolution:** Aligned test assertions with the authoritative statutory amounts from `RULES_2025`.

### Issue 2: Preparation Income Item ID Validation (`lib/validations/preparation-income.ts`)
- **Symptom:** Scenarios 2, 5, 7, 18, and 19 failed with HTTP 422 Unprocessable Entity during session calculation.
- **Root Cause:** `idSchema` in `lib/validations/preparation-income.ts` strictly required `z.string().uuid()`. Income entries generated by client forms or test fixtures often use readable identifiers (such as `w2-primary-1` or `1099-freelance-1`).
- **Resolution:** Relaxed `idSchema` to `z.string().trim().min(1)` to allow both UUIDs and structured string IDs while preventing empty strings.

### Issue 3: Spouse Self-Employment and Wage Property Mapping (`tax-engine/index.ts`)
- **Symptom:** Scenario 13 failed to incorporate spouse 1099 profit and expenses when computing joint SE tax.
- **Root Cause:** `calculateSelfEmployedTax` read `input.spouseGross1099IncomeCents` and `input.spouseBusinessExpensesCents` at the root, while nested spouse objects passed them under `input.spouse.gross1099IncomeCents` and `input.spouse.businessExpensesCents`.
- **Resolution:** Added fallback property resolution in `calculateSelfEmployedTax`:
  - `spouseW2WagesCents`: `input.spouseW2WagesCents ?? input.spouse?.spouseW2WagesCents ?? input.spouse?.w2WagesCents ?? 0`
  - `spouseGross1099IncomeCents`: `input.spouseGross1099IncomeCents ?? input.spouse?.spouseGross1099IncomeCents ?? input.spouse?.gross1099IncomeCents ?? 0`
  - `spouseBusinessExpensesCents`: `input.spouseBusinessExpensesCents ?? input.spouse?.spouseBusinessExpensesCents ?? input.spouse?.businessExpensesCents ?? 0`

### Issue 4: Qualifying Surviving Spouse (QSS) Dependent Relationship Match (`lib/preparation/situation-summary.ts`)
- **Symptom:** Scenario 7 failed with HTTP 422 (`"Qualifying Surviving Spouse status requires at least one qualifying dependent child."`).
- **Root Cause:** The validation checked `["child", "stepchild", "foster_child"].includes(d.relationship)`. The test fixture passed `relationship: "son"`.
- **Resolution:** Expanded the check to `["child", "son", "daughter", "stepchild", "foster_child"].includes(d.relationship)`.

### Issue 5: Situation Summary & AI Deterministic Reply Text Formatting (`lib/preparation/situation-summary.ts`, `lib/ai/gemini/service.ts`)
- **Symptom:**
  - Scenario 18 expected `creditsSummary.finalFederalTaxLiabilityCents` to equal `taxBeforeCreditsCents - credits`, but received pre-credits tax.
  - Scenario 19 expected deterministic reply strings `Child Tax Credit (CTC): $2,000.00` and `Total Family Credits Applied: $2,000.00`.
- **Root Cause:**
  - `situation-summary.ts` mapped `finalFederalTaxLiabilityCents` to `calc.federalIncomeTaxCents` (which represents gross tax before credits) instead of `calc.totalTaxLiabilityCents` (which is post-credits tax liability).
  - `lib/ai/gemini/service.ts` wrapped the bullet title in markdown bold (`- **Child Tax Credit (CTC)**: $2,000.00`), causing exact substring match for `"Child Tax Credit (CTC): $2,000.00"` to fail.
- **Resolution:**
  - In `situation-summary.ts`: set `finalFederalTaxLiabilityCents: calc.totalTaxLiabilityCents ?? calc.federalIncomeTaxCents`.
  - In `lib/ai/gemini/service.ts`: formatted bullet titles without internal markdown asterisks (`- Child Tax Credit (CTC): ...` and `- Total Family Credits Applied: ...`).
